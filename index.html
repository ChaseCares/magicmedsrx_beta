About M.A.G.I.C. Meds Rx

M.A.G.I.C. Meds Rx connects patients and providers with the tools and information needed to make prescription medications more affordable and accessible.

We provide guidance on drug manufacturer patient assistance programs , which can deliver medications directly to patients’ homes for up to one year completely FREE . These programs are available to both uninsured and Medicare patients.

For those with commercial insurance , we offer information on manufacturer copay cards , which can significantly reduce out-of-pocket medication costs.

Our platform also features Medicaid and VA formulary search tools , allowing users to quickly determine whether specific medications are covered under their respective programs.

Additionally, M.A.G.I.C. Meds Rx helps Medicare and Medicare Advantage beneficiaries understand their coverage options — including monthly premiums, annual deductibles, and supplemental programs such as the Medicare Prescription Payment Program and Medicare Extra Help — ensuring every individual can identify the most suitable plan and resource for their needs.
